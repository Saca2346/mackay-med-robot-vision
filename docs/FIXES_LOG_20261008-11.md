## 2. Tried and NOT adopted (so nobody repeats them)

| Idea | What was measured | Why not |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Model input 800×1280 instead of 960 | rs_uji1 replay: PICK 17.2 s vs 238.7 s (also 129 vs 340 readings earlier) | slower and worse; **960 stays** |
| OCR on OpenVINO | 2.4× faster on big crops, but 321 vs 281 ms per box in the real pipeline, no PICK | the benchmark did not match the real load |
| OCR on the MX350 GPU (CUDA) | 235 ms vs 79 ms per label on the CPU (text identical) | small crops: copying to the GPU costs more than the work |
| Whole-spine OCR forced down to 24 px | 80 spine reads, 0 Terumo REF, CPU 525 → 850 ms per box | the REF is too small to read at that distance |
| "Taken box" appearance hint after a box is put back | 207.9 s without vs 211.3 s with | the hint was already active; the box sat at the left image edge where reading is poor |
| Camera shift by nearest-box matching | test showed a 60 px shift "matches" a neighbour 10 px away | replaced by the shelf's outer edges |

## 6. Expiry labels from the server (11 Oct)

163 / 163 photos done, all version 2, 1,215 labels. 18 dates appear in 38–82 photos each (the real dates of the boxes on
the shelf); only 15 rare dates (17 labels, 1.4 %) look like digit misreads such as `2038-01-31`. For training only the
label **position** counts, so a misread date text does not hurt. Coverage per photo: 76 photos at 80–100 %, 39 at 50–79 %,
27 at 1–49 %, 21 at 0 % (all 21 are from 23 Sep). Low coverage is mostly **blurry close-ups** where the dates cannot be read
by anyone; unlabelled visible dates would teach the model to ignore them. A sample of the 50–79 % band confirmed legible dates missing
(e.g. a Sled Bag date), while 6 of 6 sampled photos at ≥ 80 % were correct.

**Decision (11 Oct):** the 76 photos at ≥ 80 % are marked reviewed (`tools/ok_expiry_batch.py`; the note says it is a coverage rule
plus a sample, not a photo-by-photo review). 87 photos stay in the pool. The 3-class model is therefore trained on **76 photos**
(775 boxes, 1,043 size labels, 719 expiry labels) and must be compared with v4 (163 photos) on `box` and `size_label` before use.
Package: `Downloads/paket_3kelas_20261011.zip`, steps in `prelabel_size_label_full/PANDUAN_TRAINING_3KELAS.md`.
